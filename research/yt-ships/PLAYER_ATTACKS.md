# Confirmed player fighter attacks and protected Earth — 2026-10-27

Follow-up: [non-Earth minefields and chained attribution](MINES.md) now remove the temporary encounter gate described in this milestone snapshot.

## Supported encounters and user flow

`ATTACK` lists current visible local enemy ships by number, alias, fighters and
shields. The caller chooses a target and committed force. Compact mode also accepts
`ATTACK <number> <fighters>`; classic main `A` enters the same dialog. Classic
planet `A` remains take-all, and compact `A` is unchanged. The list excludes self,
teammates, destroyed ships and unrevealed cloaked ships. A guessed hidden number
does not reveal its identity or equipment. No new local reveal/anti-cloak power is
invented: existing sensor detection supplies session visibility.

A final named confirmation requires a separately entered exact Y (case-insensitive).
Command-chain remainders and terminal typeahead are discarded before the prompt;
command replay and trailing-slash saving are disabled during confirmation. Blank,
N, other text, Escape, disconnect or expiration commits nothing. Deadlines are
checked after confirmation, after acquiring the store lock and after calculation;
expiry while waiting or resolving rolls back the attack. Target/force
selection may be stacked; the final approval may not. No choice survives another
attack command, reconnect, or replacement.

The store lock reloads both ships and checks attacker session generation, target
generation, co-location, life, team, visibility, available fighters and supported
aftermath before RNG. Current defenses and cloak, rather than a stale quoted
combat result, determine the outcome. Free fighter attacks and no automatic
missile counterfire follow the bounded [direct-duel observations](RECOVERY.md).
The existing bounded duel engine prevents unbounded resolution under the lock.

Damage, full salvage consumption/awards, estate transfers, session invalidation
and protected-Earth mine disposal share one transaction. Failure at any stage
aborts all of them. Reports appear only after successful storage; they describe
saved fighter losses, surviving target defenses, destruction, each inventory
award and safe mine disposal. No extra daily turns or replacement credits are
awarded by the attack path. Existing replacement construction remains login-only.

## Protected spawn and explicit incomplete-combat gate

[The original Earth chain](LOOT.md#attacker-death-in-mine-aftermath) drops the
victim's mines and damages later replacement ships. This defect is intentionally
not reproduced. Earth combat first consumes the sampled mine award from the
victim, then safely destroys its unsalvaged mine remainder. The sum of awarded
and disarmed mines equals the victim's original stock. The winner's preexisting
mines are unaffected. Unsalvaged disposal is an explicit sink; no hazardous stock
is created at Earth and no mine stock is duplicated between loot and a drop.

The modern world still has no minefield storage or explosions. Consequently a
mine-bearing target outside Earth cannot be prepared or attacked: the gate is
rechecked at commit in case another session acquired mines during confirmation.
This is a temporary feature boundary, not a claim of ordinary non-Earth DOS
minefield parity or an intentional permanent removal of mine aftermath. Supported
mine-free non-Earth duels and protected-Earth duels are now exposed. No unsafe or
silently incomplete mine-bearing encounter is exposed outside Earth.

Other death/settlement helpers remain internal, do not create minefields, and
are not new production kill commands. All future hazardous-drop paths, including
retaliation and mines generated by chained deaths, must preserve Earth protection.
No attacker post-kill mine death is synthesized in this bounded milestone.

## Validation

- Engine: exact Earth mine conservation/disposal, replay rejection, protected
  replacement/newcomer exit and unsupported non-Earth encounters rejected before
  randomness/mutation, including a target acquiring mines after preparation.
- Store: late abort after full death/loot/disposal restores all state; concurrent
  killing confirmations yield one settlement; old victim generations remain
  invalid after replacement, which preserves 37 turns.
- Session: fresh exact confirmation, blank/N/stacked answers, disconnect, hidden
  identity protection, target/actor movement and replacement, new team protection,
  new cloak, saved outcome reporting, classic A dispatch/dialog, typeahead clearing
  and command-repeat isolation.
- `scripts/smoke_attacks.py`: real GoDoors PTYs reject stacked/queued approval and
  `Y/`, then a second real game process confirms a kill while the victim is online.
  The victim's old movement chain fails; the replacement preserves remaining
  turns; a newcomer buys equipment at Earth and leaves. Saved credits conserve,
  captured titles/treasuries persist and Earth has no defense/hazard. Transcript:
  `evidence/attack-smoke.txt`. CI reruns this smoke.

Race-enabled tests, vet, existing duel/salvage model verifiers, startup/navigation/
cloak/lifecycle/salvage terminal regressions and Linux/Windows cross-builds cover
this integration. These are local/PTY/cross-build checks, not native BBS combat
acceptance or exact original RNG/MBF equivalence. No new DOS session is claimed.
