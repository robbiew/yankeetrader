# Bounded Go departed-garrison movement and same-scan activation

`ReplayDepartedStockMercenary` consumes a modeled pre-Mercenary world and explicit
calculated RNG state/source helper clocks. It returns an owned phase world and
calculated receipts, or nil world/zero statistics on error. It ends before
lottery, final metadata, public/private reports and scoring. It is not a complete
four-day Go replay or production maintenance policy. The separate held-friendly
API and historical tails retain their previous strict admission.

## Mutable descending scan

Existing Python synthesis computes the original three-day parent, applies the
declared departure fixture and finishes child economy/mobile phases before this
phase begins. Its modeled intermediate world is the fixture input. Native target
worlds and RNG witnesses never become seeds or corrections.

The phase eagerly draws once per positive physical defense, then scans the
changing physical records from sector 3000 down to 1. The local 37-fighter group
acts first at Gateway Cache. Its actual movement draw decides hold or departure;
the incoming 97337-fighter group later acts at 2368. Each departure clears force
and owner with source859e `00002800`, consumes /48cf target helpers including
same-origin rejection, and derives the complete directed BFS route. Lower-sector
arrivals are reread and activated later in that same scan. Higher-sector arrivals
have already passed the cursor and do not reactivate.

The actual incoming route stops at its gateway entry, independently of its
planned target. Cached physical force is added once before raw SINGLE INT stock
conversion. If the local group has departed, the taking actor is 97337 and its
separate 37 fighters remain outside that actor. If it held, the actor is 97374.
Empty/subunit stock clears silently with source8472 `00008000`; whole stock adds
once and fractional discard is separately recorded. Capture is true for an empty
physical gateway even when its planet governor is -2; governor/name/ground bytes
do not change. Mercenary physical conservation includes both final deployments.

## Admission and qualification

The API admits only the retained initial 37 local fighters and 97337 incoming force,
joint neutral-governor empty/half/37 stock or Mercenary-governor 37 stock, canonical
stock bytes and the actual linked mine-free zero-bank Gateway Cache identity.
Default geometry, inactive humans, zero taxes, bounded whole NPC forces and
integer graph are checked. Helper clocks remain ordered first-minute family-one
/48cf inputs. Other mines, merges, defenses, planet pickups or changed local
force are refused; the mutable scan is bounded to 16 activations and each target
selection to 64 attempts. No branch is chosen from a captured route or endpoint.

All 24 modeled worlds and complete receipts match Python: 66 activations include
18 later local activations, 15 captured pickups and 9 held counter-controls.
Tests compare ordered gates/reseeds/candidates/routes/entered prefixes, whole
worlds, final physical locations/totals, pickup actors/fractions and raw residues.
Changed bytes are constrained to actual activation origins/destinations plus the
stock field. Inputs and outputs retain independent storage. Exhausted or invalid
late clocks, unreachable later routes and later mine/planet/defense contexts fail
atomically even after internal pickup writes. Historical held-only/tail refusal
tests remain in place.

The exporter reuses both existing departure corpora, including the two-case zero
stock supplement, their frozen acquisition sources and original static byte
audit. Independent four-day qualification remains 144 complete six-file
comparisons /864 native files and 1152 child RNG witnesses. No native acquisition,
