post-detach liveness check despite a valid flush message. Its final metadata

promoted to completed observations. The six valid partial-case runs remain
supplementary controls and their 36 output files match the final first child.

Unchanged retries then stopped. The persisted receipt addresses acknowledgement
persistence; it is not claimed to repair the second liveness-check race.

exit-completion protocol**: it writes and closes the full trace, verifies the
actual UTF8 byte count, then synchronously writes/closes a receipt. The controller
requires exact canonical trace/receipt schemas, byte lengths, event/error/drop
counts, natural zero exit and fresh paths. Any delivered asynchronous acknowledgement
must agree exactly; duplicate or conflicting messages fail. Missing/truncated
receipts, partial writes, script errors, nonzero exit and dropped events fail.
The entire pre-flush DOS event-acquisition body and exit-hook suffix are
byte-identical to the old observer. The original deadline and owned cleanup
remain enabled. All 17 portable protocol regressions pass.
