# Surviving Xannor defenders and uncollected planet stock

This acquisition slice follows an audit of the existing gateway, evolved-combat,
survivor-chain, friendly, departed and neutral-owner controls. Those already
cover Mercenaries winning a linked-planet offensive battle, and mine deaths
before battle. They do not establish the new paired losing-offense/defender-stock
continuation. PR #112's before-only owner/mine replay remains a separate slice.

Four recipes were frozen before native launch; every outcome is retained without
retry or edits to fit a winner. The balanced case was a counter-control with no
promised winner. All 24 initial per-mode fixture preparations passed before the
first native launch. Each case applies fifteen declared data edits to its pinned
November 4 evolved-combat parent, runs November 5 at 00:00:02, then inherits all
six exact outputs for November 6 at 00:00:02 without fixture edits or repairs.
Two unobserved, three observed and one attach-only mode are retained per day.

There are 48 complete original-DOS runs / 288 output files. All six modes agree
within each case/day. The first-stage ledger accounts for 24 transformed data
links and 120 unchanged side-file links; day two adds 144 unchanged exact links.
Original guests, host/CPU, commands, clocks, raw returns, process lifecycle,
loaded observer and persisted flush receipts are independently verified.
