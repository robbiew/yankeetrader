## Source progression and effects

The phase eagerly draws once per positive physical defense. A surviving attacker
at 2368 then consumes its movement gate, clears force/owner with source859e
`00002800`, reseeds helper 1 /48cf for each target attempt and derives the directed
BFS route from the actual graph. Its actual first hop must be the linked Xannor
defense at Gateway Cache sector 3000. Only this fatal prefix is entered.

Negative owner selects combat even though the ordinary eager gate draw is still
consumed. Each admitted attacker has at most 37 fighters, so the original
both-sides-over-DOUBLE200 batch 150 branch cannot occur. Successive source RNG
draws subtract one casualty from a side until one reaches zero. Only attacker
extinction with positive defenders is qualified. A computed attacker victory is
refused atomically rather than rewritten to a captured loser.

The only phase writes are the original departure force/owner pair and surviving
gateway defender count. Xannor owner, planet stock/governor/ground and unrelated
bytes remain unchanged; no stale Xannor group-pointer cache is cleared. There
is no pickup after this fatal encounter. On the second child, Mercenary force
is already extinct: only physical-field draws execute, no movement/helper/battle
receipt is created, and every world byte including the inherited departure pair
is copied unchanged.

Admission keeps default geometry, inactive humans, zero taxes, canonical declared
stock/governor, linked mine-free gateway identity, bounded whole physical NPC
forces and integer graph. First-phase before pairs are the four exact declared
small-attacker contexts; next-phase pairs retain the measured survivors and
post-economy stock 40/3 with exact `00002800` origin residues. Helpers are bounded
ordered first-minute family 1 /48cf clocks. Active players, other origins, hazards,
unmeasured combat outcomes or surplus/exhausted clocks fail without publication.
