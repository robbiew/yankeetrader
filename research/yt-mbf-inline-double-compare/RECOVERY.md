# Bounded inline DOUBLE comparison /a6

All source evidence already existed. The retained comparison packet contains
/a6 at a818: it calls the shared 1ec3 inline resolver, selects right DOUBLE FAC
0016, then uses the qualified four-word comparison core. The direct DOUBLE and
inline-add verifiers authenticate the complete wrapper/core/ABI, resolver tail,
descriptor helper and original caller metadata. This supplement checks /a6's
actual dispatch and wrapper. No new source query was needed.

Seven /a6 sites in the bounded maintenance listing match original bytes, using
selectors 80 and 81. The immutable direct DOUBLE inventory records these sites
separately. This admits static caller evidence, not observed execution or an
exhaustive indirect inventory.

The Go model composes `SingleAddInlineOffset` with `CompareDouble` after a safe
eight-byte operand read. Selected memory is left and the entire DOUBLE FAC is
right. The shared resolver discards the selector's high bit, uses stride eight,
selects caller CS:0012 for descriptor 7a62 and CS:0010 for every other word, and
wraps the offset at sixteen bits. All four comparison words and 56 significand
bits are retained. No second arithmetic algorithm or gameplay caller is added.

## Native controls

All 348 first attempts returned normally. The 329 full comparisons comprise
154 ordered direct-control pairs under each descriptor tag, ten extra selector
controls, eight alternate RNG/initial-flag runs with declared 16/32-byte frame
padding, one fallback tag and two last-safe eight-byte spans at fff8. They cover
zero residues, both signs, exponent extremes, equality, fraction bits through 54,
subtraction overflow/parity at every word and higher-word precedence.

The 19 separate resolver-only controls cover selector aliases, wrapping offsets,
fallback and the original 7e00/08c3 caller header with selectors 80/81 under both
tag paths. Only these controls replace the post-resolver `MOV DI,0016` with RETF;
they predict offsets without claiming an operand read or comparison flags.

I6CF is a separate eight-byte protocol: 113 bytes for full controls, 97 for
resolver-only controls. It retains actual descriptor/header/frame metadata,
selector, requested/actual initial flags, immediate result FLAGS, sixteen actual
native Jcc outcomes, nine registers, full FAC, RNG and mode. Full controls also
retain the selected operand and distinct eight-byte alternate-base poison.
Branches execute with only MOV/Jcc instructions after immediate PUSHF capture;
flags and branch outcomes are not synthesized by reloading saved flags.

Only CF/PF/ZF/SF/OF (mask 08c5) are predicted. Signed CPU conditions are not
numeric MBF ordering, and AF remains outside the semantic contract. Complete
raw FLAGS are fixed observations even where no prediction is admitted. ES=DS,
clear DF, declared synthetic SS:BP-2 descriptor frames and safe disjoint full
reads are explicit bounds. Wrapped offsets remain resolver-only. The returned
SI is the resolved offset; full DI is 0016 and resolver-only DI is the offset.
FAC, selected/poison memory, other captured registers, RNG and mode are unchanged.

## Provenance and validation

The acquisition source and recipe were frozen before launch. Fixed pins preserve
all first observations and frozen source. The independent verifier reconstructs
complete copied executables/diagnostics, excludes loader relocations, validates
actual commands, shell return, pinned host/date/time and exact raw protocols,
then binds the portable fixture to every native trace. Corruption tests reject
lost flags/branches, protocol relabeling, altered readonly inputs, wrong resolver
metadata, changed original observations and dropped fixture controls.

integration, general unwind policy and native Windows/live-BBS acceptance remain
separate. Gameplay, save format 35, source fingerprints and sysop procedures are
unchanged. Earlier direct and inline SINGLE comparison milestones remain intact.
